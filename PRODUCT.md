# WePrompt — Brand Context for Impeccable

## Project
WePrompt is the first AI solutions marketplace in Latin America. A B2B platform connecting AI solution creators with Brazilian businesses (PMEs).

## Target Audience
- Primary: Brazilian SMEs (small and medium businesses) looking for ready-to-use AI solutions
- Secondary: AI creators (developers, automation specialists) who want to monetize their solutions

## Brand Personality
- Professional and trustworthy, not corporate
- Modern and direct, not trendy
- Premium but accessible
- Brazilian identity (PT-BR, payments in BRL, local support)

## Visual Identity
- Primary color: #2563EB (vibrant blue)
- Dark: #111827
- Background: white / #F8FAFC
- NO purple gradients
- NO generic Inter/Roboto fonts (use DM Sans already imported)
- NO emoji icons (SVG only)
- NO generic SaaS card layouts

## Tone
Confident, clear, warm. Like a trusted Brazilian tech partner.

## What makes WePrompt memorable
- Every solution is curated and tested before listing
- Full Portuguese support
- Payments in BRL via PIX or card
- First mover in Latin America AI marketplace space

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Purple/violet color schemes
- Generic "Get Started" blue buttons
- Roboto/Inter/Arial fonts
- Nested white cards on white backgrounds
- Generic stock photo aesthetics
- "AI slop" gradients
